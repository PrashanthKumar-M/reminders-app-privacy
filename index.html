Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

Reminders APP is a reminder application developed by Prashanth Kumar.

1. Data Collection

The app does not collect, sell, or share personal information with third parties.

2. Reminder Data

Reminders created by the user are stored locally on the user's device and are used only to provide the reminder functionality.

3. Notifications

The app may schedule notifications to remind users about reminders they have created.

4. App Permissions

The app may request permission to allow alarms and reminders while launching the app. This permission is used to schedule and deliver reminders created by the user.

5. Internet

The app does not transmit reminder data to a remote server.

6. Third-Party Services

The current version of the app does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

7. Data Security

Data stored locally by the app is protected by the security mechanisms provided by the Android operating system.

8. Data Deletion

Users can delete reminders from the application. Uninstalling the application removes its locally stored application data, subject to Android and device behavior.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be made available through the privacy policy page associated with the application.
